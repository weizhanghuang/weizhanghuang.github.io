Call for papers: Special Issue of Journal of Mathematics Study on Adaptive Moving Mesh Methods

We are pleased to announce an upcoming special issue of Journal of Mathematical Study on Adaptive Moving Mesh Methods. We invite authors to submit their original, full length research articles using the article submission at here by December 31, 2014. Please indicate "MMM special issue" in the memo during submission. All articles will undergo a rigorous refereeing process by experts in the field.

Please feel free to contact us should you have questions.
