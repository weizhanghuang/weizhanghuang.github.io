Weizhang Huang's research

Research topics:

• Adaptive moving mesh methods • Anisotropic mesh adaptation and generation • Finite element methods, finite difference methods, discontinuous Galerkin (DG) methods, finite volumen methods, spectral methods, collocation methods • Geometric integration, numerical solution of Hamiltonian systems • Computational fluid dynamics • fractional differential equations
